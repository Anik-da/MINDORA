    Voice & sound
    Voice is opt-in. Speech input uses the packaged Chromium speech recognition API and may use an external recognition service; system speech voices provide spoken output. MINDORA does not retain audio.
    
          
    Local screen awareness
    Off by default. When enabled, MINDORA periodically captures the foreground app locally for OCR-based activity classification. Screenshots and recognized text are not saved or sent to Gemini/TinyFish. Private browsing and detected sensitive fields suppress suggestions.
    Screen awareness is off.
    Last local classification: No observation yet.
    
          
    Proactive help
    Suggestions are tentative and only use local app/OCR signals. MINDORA never clicks or takes action without your request.
    
          
    Windows inactivity lock
    Optional Windows-only device lock. Disabled by default; at least five minutes of system inactivity and a visible cancel warning are required. Ten seconds of inactivity changes only the companion pose.
    
          
    Desktop privacy
    Desktop observation stays inside the authorized desktop application. The web companion does not have screen capture access.
    
          
    Browser profiles
    Profiles are managed by TinyFish and remain on the trusted application side.
